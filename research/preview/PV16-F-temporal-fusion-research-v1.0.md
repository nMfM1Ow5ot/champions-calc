2026-11-26 JST. LIMITED_DEVELOPMENT_RESULT, independent acceptance blocked. GPT basic principles v1.10, IMRaD structure.
## Introduction
A selection roster is invariant within an authenticated scene/session with stable slots, but single-frame appearance scores vary. Compare simple rank aggregation before introducing a learned temporal model. Reference: Dwork et al. (2001), Rank Aggregation Methods for the Web, https://archives.iw3c2.org/www10/cdrom/papers/577/index.html; Borda positional aggregation adapted here. No claim of independent-frame likelihood or calibrated probabilities.

Compare arithmetic mean distance, median distance, mean rank (Borda, average ranks for ties), plurality of first places (ties split, Borda then ID tie-break). Distances are not probabilities. Group by caller-validated session/slot, exact frame-content digest deduplication, reject conflicting repeats, mixed sessions, incomplete candidate sets or invalid scores; missing slot contributes no score; all outputs accepted=false. Mean distance cumulative first-session counts 5,6,6,6,6; other three 5,6,5,6,6. Second session all cumulative counts 6. Every method/session leave-one-frame-out replay remains 6/6 (44 runs total). All eight method/session order and duplicate invariance checks pass. Seven synthetic contract cases pass (tie, empty, missing, mixed session, conflicting duplicate, NaN, incomplete candidates). Both JSON files byte-identical on full rerun. Mean-distance candidate simpler and stable on this data; all four tie on final accuracy. Not a statistically justified superiority claim. First two sampled detections are separated by 10 seconds in both recordings; no real-time latency measurement or required minimum frame-count established.
## Discussion
Two previously examined recordings, nonblind provisional labels, overlapping training assets and correlated observations; not independent evidence (eligible n=0). Scores may share systematic bias, no guarantee aggregation improves a future roster. Duplicate invariance only for exact stable frame ID; adjacent correlated frames remain correlated. No posterior probability, hard exclusions, calibrated acceptance, scene detector, negative challenge or production integration. Do not use the result for deterministic observation. Candidate mean distance for future development; compare on fresh, independent battles and realistic sampling cadence, establish identity/reset and conflict/abstention contracts. Continue shape/source-label validation and calibration.
