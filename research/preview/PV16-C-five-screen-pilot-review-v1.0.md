## Frozen reported predictions vs user corrections
The user verified all 30 slots one screen at a time. Screen 2 initial all-correct response was explicitly retracted and superseded by one correction; no correction was used to tune scoring for that screen. Correct counts: 6,5,4,6,5. Top-1 26/30 = 86.6667%; error 4/30; complete screens 2/5 = 40%. Two additional errors had rank 2, one had unrecorded full rank, and one true label was absent from the screen-5 dictionary. Thus reported Top-2 and Top-3 are both bounded by 28/30 to 29/30, conditional on the previous reported ranks and candidate sets; neither exact value nor MRR recoverable. Do not equate template-level ranking with calibrated confidence. These are descriptive sample statistics, not population estimates.

## Method and evidence level
Visual preselection of small changing candidate dictionaries (reported 8,8,10,9,10 reference images), manually selected slot geometry, no type correction, no duplicate-species constraint. PV13-like distance = 256-bit dHash Hamming/256 + 0.15*abs(log(aspect ratio ratio)) + 0.25*abs(density difference) + 0.7*Hellinger(normalized 24-bin hue histograms). PNG references segmented with alpha; screen crops segmented with background-color difference and largest connected component; resize and hue rounding differ by implementation. This is a development diagnostic, not the approved controlled A/B/C comparison. Ground truth supplied by user, not a verification of raw battle records. Not a blind or fixed-universe test; independence of recordings and rights eligibility not established.
In the final review, the two surviving local scripts for screens 4 and 5 were rerun using the exact resized inputs and embedded descriptors: all 12 rank-1 predictions and four-decimal scores, plus screen 5 slot 1 top 3, matched the reported values. Full candidate rankings for these two screens and hashes/source mappings are retained privately. For screens 1–3, the reported predictions and partial rankings are recorded, but original score scripts/full output not recovered; do not claim independent computational replay. No formal production accuracy acceptance.

- Screen 3: two true labels at rank 2; score gaps 0.0717 and 0.0017 (rounded reported scores), respectively. Same wrong label chosen twice. Near-tie particularly unstable. Candidate inclusion alone insufficient.
- Screen 5: verified missing true label in surviving 10-reference script; not a matching error conditional on presence. PokéAPI actually has the needed icon 687.png (blob 6d76e793a2669f3f6d65576a0218f3b59d7e3cbb); checked after user correction. https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/blob/master/sprites/pokemon/versions/generation-ix/champions/icons/687.png . Do not retroactively revise predictions or claim recovery on re-ranking. The wrong label duplicates another slot.
- Three errors on two screens involved duplicate predictions (not three separate legality violations); constraint enforcement could remove these conflicts if the applicable species clause is confirmed, but cannot rescue a missing true candidate. No proof all errors are resolvable. Correct type evidence would distinguish the erroneous pairs; classifier itself not tested here.
- At least one candidate omission confirmed; two ranking failures confirmed; one remaining cause unresolved. Preselection biases accuracy upward, while omissions can lower it. No comparative baseline, no significance test, no attribution of accuracy to PokéAPI.
