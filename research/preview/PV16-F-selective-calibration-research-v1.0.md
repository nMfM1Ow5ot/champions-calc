## Introduction
Selective classification (Geifman & El-Yaniv 2017 https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08500); Learn then Test (https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01052); risk-controlling prediction sets (https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.02703). Separate candidate ranking from confirmed evidence. No confidence probability is inherent in heuristic distance.

## Methods
Replayed previous joint25_dynamic ranker with pinned source hashes and prior group-excluded banks: 240 slots, 239 evaluable (96 exact-ID and 143 provisional base-species labels), 40 screens, 29 proxy roster groups; prior top and rank reproduced. Extra cohort top/rank comparison uses base identity, not full form verification. Margin = second ID distance minus first ID distance. Test five margin thresholds .025/.05/.1/.2/.4, five distance thresholds .5/.65/.8/.95/1.1 and 25 joint combinations; strict positive margin even for distance-only. Select within family maximal accepted count subject to zero observed errors, ties lower margin then greater distance; no qualifying margin-only rule => reject all. Repeat threshold selection leaving each proxy group out. This is exploratory and not independent (prior bank design, labels and other choices already used the cohort). Unknown proxy removes true ID or all true-base IDs from the ranking, then scores the remaining list, not a genuine unknown image, not operational full-catalog gate. Probability pilot: group-equal-weight Laplace-smoothed constant and six fixed margin bins, fit on other proxy groups, score held-out groups via Brier loss. Apply selected thresholds illustratively to prior full mean-distance fused video ranks; not separate temporal calibration.
Mathematical decision: accept candidate only when d1<=tau, d2-d1>=gamma and d2>d1, valid context, complete verified candidate catalog, frozen calibrated rule, and resolved visual identity. Development module exposes would_accept only, accepted always false. Conditional risk P(error|accept) and coverage P(accept) reported separately. For fixed predeclared rule on independent representative battles, count accepted battles, define error as any wrong accepted slot; conditional accepted-battle Bernoulli risk with zero observed errors has one-sided 95% upper bound 1-0.05^(1/n); 59, 299, 2995 accepted independent battles required for bounds <=5%,1%,0.1%. Verify coverage independently; no multiple-threshold selection on final test. No claim of zero population risk or independent frame labels.

## Discussion
Joint improves simulated unknown rejection by one over distance but loses one correct known candidate; neither satisfies >=95% coverage nor zero unknown false acceptance. Margin-only cannot achieve nonzero zero-error coverage on tested grid. Prior official independent eligible n=0; provisional labels, visual aliases, nonindependent proxy groups, ranker/dictionary dependencies, and missing actual negatives remain. Keep both distance and joint as research candidates only. Freeze rule, scene identity, exact labels, catalog/version, sample cadence and negative cohorts before independent evaluation; tune coverage and temporal features in development first. Do not use uncertain labels as hard constraints, or score as a likelihood. No approved risk target changed; 5/1/.1% examples only. No main or production changes; no Codex, paid API, or new asset downloads.
